# 3.14.0-pod.1.2.1 — official 3.14.0 base

The fork now builds on the official TigerScale V3 3.14.0. TigerPOD is unchanged (still POD 1.2.1).

- The scale is ready to weigh in about three seconds instead of twenty: the logo still shows for two seconds, the rest of the start-up happens behind it, and the Wi-Fi connects in the background.
- Choosing a Wi-Fi network in Settings no longer restarts the scale, and you stay signed in to your account.
- A scale switched on before its router, or whose router restarts, now reconnects on its own every 30 seconds.
- The on-screen keyboard follows the language: AZERTY in French, QWERTZ in German, QWERTY elsewhere.
- Documentation: the account is required to weigh (official docs from 11 November).

Known issue reported upstream: on one bench unit the right-hand reader cannot read 4-byte MIFARE Classic cards, while 7-byte tags (TigerTag) read normally. It looks like a hardware fault on that module.

Tested on a real 3.5B scale. Not yet validated on the 3.5 without the B.
